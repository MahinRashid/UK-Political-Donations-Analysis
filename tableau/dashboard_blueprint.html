Who Funds UK Political Parties? Dashboard blueprint

Two dashboards built from the Electoral Commission donations register, 2001–2019. Every number is from the cleaned data. Orange markers on each dashboard match the build cards below it.

Palette:

Design rules for both dashboards

Titles state the finding

"SNP, Labour and UKIP get over half their money from 10 donors", not "Top 10 share by party".

Big numbers first

A row of 4 BANs (big-number text sheets) answers "how much?" before the charts answer "why?"

Fonts

Format → Workbook: Tableau Semibold for titles (14pt), Tableau Book for everything else (11pt labels, 9pt axes).

Tiled layout

Fixed size 1200 × 800. Horizontal and vertical containers, 8px outer padding, white sheet backgrounds on a #F3F5F7 dashboard.

Labels, not gridlines

Show mark labels. On bar charts, remove gridlines, zero lines and the value axis.

Neutral wording

Describe patterns, never judge a party or donor. The subject is political, so neutrality is part of the quality.
